What a parent wants to know before handing over the tablet.

  • No ads, no buying for kids

    Only a grown-up pays, behind a parent lock.

  • We collect no child data

    Only the parent has an account. The child's name never leaves the device.

  • There is no way to fail

    Mistakes take nothing away. The lesson always reaches the end.

  • Works without internet

    Everything is inside the app: planes, cars and grandma's house.

  • A daily time limit

    15, 20 or 30 minutes. When time is up, the stickers go to sleep.

    English, Russian or Ukrainian. Up to 4 children in one account.

Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

It is built around a five-year-old and works well from 4 to 6.

Does my child need to read?

No. There is no text to read in the game: every instruction is spoken and every button is a picture.

Which devices does it work on?

Tablets and phones, held sideways. Today it plays in the browser; Android and iPhone apps are on the way.

Does it need the internet?

The app works fully offline. The browser version needs a connection the first time it loads.

What does it cost?

The first theme is free for good: 5 lessons, 12 stickers and the Farm album. The other themes will open with a subscription that a parent buys inside the app. Prices will be published at launch.

Are there ads?

No ads, no links out and nothing to buy on the child's side of the app.

What do you know about my child?

Nothing personal. A parent account is optional and only carries progress to another device. The child's name stays on the device.

Can I limit screen time?

Yes. In the parents' area you can set 15, 20 or 30 minutes a day.

How long should a child play?

A good session is 8–15 minutes: two or three lessons and some time with the album.

Can two children share one tablet?

Yes, up to 4 children, each with their own coins, stickers and albums.
